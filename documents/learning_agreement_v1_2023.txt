Legacy submission method
Students may email an unsigned Learning Agreement draft to the exchange mailbox. Email receipt is considered sufficient to begin academic review. Digital signatures are optional.

Changes during mobility
Course changes may be summarized in the body of an email. A revised Learning Agreement is recommended but not required.

Document status
This guide has been superseded and is preserved only to demonstrate version conflicts. It must not be used for current submissions.
