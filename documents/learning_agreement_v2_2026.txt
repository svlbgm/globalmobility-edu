Initial submission
The student uploads the Learning Agreement to the university mobility portal. The document must contain the student signature, the home-institution academic approval, and the host-institution approval. An email attachment alone does not constitute a completed submission.

Changes during mobility
When the course list changes, the student uploads a revised During Mobility section and an updated course-mapping table. The Academic Advisor checks academic compatibility. The International Programs Office verifies signatures and document completeness.

Final submission
After mobility, the student uploads the signed After Mobility document and final transcript. The Department Administrator may return an incomplete package without forwarding it for recognition.

Processing time
No guaranteed response or completion time is stated in this guide.
