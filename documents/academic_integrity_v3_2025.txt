General rule
Students must follow the assessment-specific instructions issued by the course instructor. Generative AI use is permitted only when the assessment instructions allow it.

Disclosure
When AI assistance is permitted, the student must describe the tool and the way it was used. AI-generated text, code, images, or analysis must not be represented as entirely unaided work.

Unclear instructions
If the assessment instructions do not state whether AI use is allowed, the student must ask the course instructor before using it. GlobalMobility EDU must not infer permission from silence.

Decision authority
The course instructor determines the permitted use for an assessment. Formal misconduct decisions follow the university disciplinary process; an automated assistant cannot make that decision.
