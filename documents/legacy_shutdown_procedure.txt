OWNER: Former IT Operations Team
LAST REVIEWED: March 2021

If a workstation displays signs of a serious virus infection, the user should immediately shut down the computer by holding the power button. The computer should remain powered off until a technician is available. The user should then inform a local supervisor by email.

This procedure was written before the organization's current cybersecurity incident playbook. It may contain instructions that conflict with newer evidence-preservation requirements. Employees and managers should verify whether a more recently approved procedure exists before following this document.
