Purpose
This procedure describes how students request recognition of courses completed during an international exchange.

Legacy procedure
Students may wait until they return from exchange before requesting course recognition. The student submits the host-university transcript, course descriptions, and a personal equivalency proposal to the department administrator. Department approval may be requested after the exchange has ended.

Approval
The department administrator forwards the request to the department committee. Courses are added to the student record after committee approval.

Document status
This procedure is retained only for historical comparison. It was replaced by Exchange Course Recognition Procedure version 2.0 and must not be used for new applications.
